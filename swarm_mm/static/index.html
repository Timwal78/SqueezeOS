How swarm market making works

Solo limit orders are lonely guesses. A swarm market making layer aggregates intent across participants (live cohort or paper swarm) and publishes a maker-style ladder plus venue weights. On the Signal path you still execute in your Alpaca, Tradier, or Interactive Brokers account.

  1. Intent in — size/side pressure across the cohort.
  2. Ladder engine — resting limits around mid; confidence decays with depth.
  3. Venue map — weight maker-rebate oriented venues.
  4. Signal out — HTTP, WebSocket, MCP, broker-native previews.
  5. You execute — no pooled book; SML does not submit the live order for you on Variant A.
